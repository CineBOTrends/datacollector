The highly anticipated #Varanasi, starring Superstar Mahesh Babu, is progressing steadily towards its worldwide theatrical release on April 7, 2027.

Mahesh Babu, who plays Rudhra, is expected to complete his current shooting portions within the next four to five days. His schedule is now nearing completion as the team continues filming the remaining portions of the project.

Meanwhile, Priyanka Chopra Jonas, who portrays Mandakini, and Prithviraj Sukumaran, who plays Kumbha, have completed their respective shooting portions for the film.

The overall shoot is expected to continue until the last week of November, with a few sequences involving other members of the cast still pending.

Alongside the ongoing shoot, the film’s extensive VFX work is progressing in parallel. The team is working on the visual-effects process alongside production, allowing completed portions to move into the VFX pipeline while filming continues.

Director S. S. Rajamouli is said to be taking a particularly careful and focused approach to the production of #Varanasi. The emphasis is reportedly on getting the required material right during the planned shoot itself, with the team looking to avoid extensive reshoot requirements later in the process.

This approach also reflects the scale of the project, where live-action footage and VFX-heavy sequences need to work together closely. By progressing the VFX work alongside the shoot, the team has the opportunity to assess the material and visual requirements well before the final stages of production.

With filming entering its final stretch, attention is also turning towards the film’s promotional campaign. Promotional content is being planned well ahead of the release, with the makers reportedly preparing a large-scale campaign.

The promotional activities are expected to begin in December, with the initial focus reportedly being on overseas markets. This will mark the beginning of the wider promotional journey leading up to the film’s April 2027 theatrical release.

With Mahesh Babu nearing completion of his schedule, the major portions of Priyanka Chopra Jonas and Prithviraj Sukumaran already wrapped, VFX progressing alongside production and promotions lined up from December, #Varanasi is steadily moving towards its final production phase.

Rudhra arrives worldwide on April 7, 2027. 🎯
